About Me

Over the past years, I've had the opportunity to explore many areas of software development even outside of professional work. From game development and interactive simulations to full-stack web development and cloud applications and even embedded systems. I am always curious to understand and build things which drives me to go over many areas in my field.

I've always had a strong hunger to learn, experiment, and grow across different areas of development.
I know that mastering many disciplines takes time, but I believe consistency matters more than speed and my committment to continuous learning will help me achieve my goals.

Philosophy

I'd rather understand how something works from the ground up than treat it as a black box. That mindset shapes how I build.
Starting from fundamentals, staying curious about the layer below the one I'm working in, and treating every project as a chance to actually learn something, not just finish it.

My Approach To Life

I believe that I don't need to have everything figured out to start moving forward. I'd rather explore, try new things, make mistakes, and learn along the way.

"Life is a journey, not a destination."

Everyone has a purpose in life, and I don't believe I need to know mine right away. What matters the most is knowing that I am moving in the right direction and surrounding myself with the right people along the way.
I will find my purpose as I continue to grow while enjoying the journey and making the most of the life I'm living.
