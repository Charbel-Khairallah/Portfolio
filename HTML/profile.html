About Me

A closer look at who I am, what I've built, and how I approach problems.
I care about writing code that's clear, systems that hold up under real use, and learning by actually shipping things — whether that's a game prototype, a piece of firmware, or a website.

Philosophy

I'd rather understand how something works from the ground up than treat it as a black box. That mindset shapes how I build — starting from fundamentals, staying curious about the layer below the one I'm working in, and treating every project as a chance to actually learn something, not just finish it.
