About Me

I'm Charbel, a Software Engineer who likes moving between disciplines rather than staying in one lane. Most of my time these days goes into building games and interactive simulations in Unity, where I enjoy the mix of systems design, math, and the immediate feedback of seeing something come alive on screen.

Outside of game development, I'm drawn to embedded systems and the layer where software meets hardware — there's something satisfying about writing code that has to work within real constraints. I've also picked up practical, hands-on experience building websites through personal and team projects, which keeps me comfortable across the stack rather than boxed into one type of problem.

What ties it together is curiosity: I'd rather understand how something works from the ground up than treat it as a black box, and I try to bring that mindset into every project I take on.
